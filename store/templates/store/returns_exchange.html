Step by Step

How to Initiate a Return

01

Initiate Return

Head over to your My Orders page, locate the eligible order, and hit the Request Return button. Alternatively, you can email us at zaviiiluxe@gmail.com with your details.

02

Get Approval

Our team will review your request and send you a Return Merchandise Authorisation (RMA) number and return shipping label within 24 hours.

03

Pack & Ship

Repackage the item(s) securely in the original packaging if possible. Attach the provided return label and drop it off at your nearest courier partner.

04

Receive Refund / Exchange

Once we receive and inspect the item, we'll process your refund or dispatch the exchange item. Refunds land in 5–7 business days.

Eligible for Return

  • Items in original, unworn condition
  • Items with all original tags attached
  • Items in original packaging
  • Defective or damaged items (any time)
  • Wrong item received
  • Requests raised within 7 days of delivery

Not Eligible for Return

  • Items that have been worn, washed, or altered
  • Items without original tags or packaging
  • Intimate apparel and swimwear (hygiene reasons)
  • Customised or personalised items
  • Items purchased during final sale events
  • Requests raised after 7 days of delivery

Exchange Policy

We offer free size and colour exchanges on all eligible items within 7 days of delivery. Simply select your preferred replacement during the exchange request process. If there is a price difference, you will be charged or refunded accordingly. Exchange shipments are dispatched within 2–3 business days after we receive the returned item.
