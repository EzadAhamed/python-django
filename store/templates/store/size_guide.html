Size Guide

Use our comprehensive size guide to find the perfect fit for every style and occasion.

Use a Measuring Tape

Always use a soft measuring tape, not a metal ruler. Hold it snugly but not tight.

Measure Over Underwear

Take measurements over light undergarments for accuracy. Avoid measuring over thick clothing.

Stand Naturally

Stand straight and relaxed — no sucking in! Natural posture gives the most accurate measurements.

Measure More Than Once

Take each measurement twice for accuracy. If between sizes, size up for a more comfortable fit.

Step by Step

How to Measure

Chest / Bust

Measure around the fullest part of your chest/bust, keeping the tape parallel to the floor. Keep arms relaxed at your sides.

Waist

Measure around your natural waistline — the narrowest part of your torso, typically above your belly button.

Hips

Stand with feet together and measure around the fullest part of your hips, approximately 20 cm below your natural waist.

Inseam

Measure from the crotch seam to the bottom of the leg along the inner seam. Best done with a pair of well-fitting trousers.
